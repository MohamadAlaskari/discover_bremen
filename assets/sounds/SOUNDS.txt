Discover Bremen - The Fifth Musician: sound set
Format: MP3 192 kbps, 44.1 kHz. band_1..band_4 are stereo, all other files mono.
Band song: G major, 96 BPM, 4/4, chords | G | C | D | G |, 4 bars plus ring-out. All four band files
have identical length and timing; each animal adds one layer: donkey = lute, dog = kettledrums,
cat = night music (serenade), rooster = 'a good voice' (brass call).
